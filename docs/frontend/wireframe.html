PropLock: screen-by-screen wireframes

This sets structure, content and behaviour for every screen of the app: tenant, broker and agency manager across Features 1 and 4. It is grey on purpose. The visual style (colour, type, imagery, radius, motion) comes from the Pinterest inspiration the designer supplies. The Pinterest patterns used here are a masonry feed of image-first "pins", a close-up page, boards, a big rounded search bar, a "+ Create" button and a mobile bottom tab bar. Each screen lists its route, its data source (the existing API) and numbered notes. The full detail is in plans/pending/frontend-redesign.md.

  1. One search field, three jobs. Tenant: search verified flats. Broker and manager: the ledger copilot (POST /ledger/copilot) when the text is a question, otherwise a filter over their own listings. The reply opens in a sheet under the field and always says it is read-only.
